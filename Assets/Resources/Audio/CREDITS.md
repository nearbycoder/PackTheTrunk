# Sound

All sound in this folder is public domain (CC0 1.0) or was made for this game.

| Files | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ui_*`, `pencil_*`, `check`, `message`, `bubble` | [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) and [UI Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio) by Kenney | CC0 |
| `land_*`, `stamp` | [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) by Kenney | CC0 |
| `cloth_*`, `leather_*`, `page_*`, `slam_*`, `latch`, `creak` | [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) by Kenney | CC0 |
| `Ambience/birds`, `Ambience/wind` | [Park ambiences](https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences) by Thimras | CC0 |
| `trip_complete`, `chapter`, `confirm`, `star_*`, `whoosh_*`, `swish`, `honk`, `engine`, `popper`, `nope` | Synthesized for Pack The Trunk (`Tools/audio/render_stingers.py`) | CC0 |

Clips were trimmed and peak-normalised to -3 dBFS; the ambience recordings were cut into
seamless loops with an equal-power crossfade (`Tools/audio/build_sfx.sh`).
